# Shooting test (tester build)

The real Pineapple Defense game in its Test Lab build. Open the page, tap Test Lab, then "Tap vs swipe cannon (2 min)". Two 60 second rounds on Stage 2 (Tap to fire and Swipe to fire, the Royal Smash style physics cannon: mechanics and feel only), Fun and Easy or Hard after each, then a Copy results button. Also "Where to hit: huddles" (tap or swipe): three penguin huddles, a limited set of five pineapple types shown in order at the top (Classic, Bouncer, Blaster, Heavy, Dart), and a bonus for unused ones. Nothing is sent or saved outside this tester's own save slot. Copyright (c) 2026 LFC Foundry. MIT License.
