## 1. Question, scope and deliberate compromise

Does correcting a visible landing point improve control over the **unchanged original flick behavior**, and does one optional cart opportunity add a repeatable defensive decision beyond that control improvement?

One Classic pineapple type, one broad support/chock, one cart on one straight rail, four basic Rockhoppers and one defended stand. Unlimited Classic throws retain the original reload; “one projectile” means one type, not a new one-shot-at-a-time restriction. Use one encounter with two timing presets, not multiple environments or levels. No Lobber, Bowling, Mega, power selector, ricochet, splash, general destruction, extra chain, helper, pickup, upgrade, Champion, trophy or saved progression.

**Chosen compromise:** retain original airborne animation and endpoint collision for all conditions. The drawn flight is a noncolliding transit; the first eligible projectile contact is the landing resolution. Only the cart has continuous swept contact. This experiment does not validate mid-flight interception, 3D ballistics, material physics or the whole playground. Removing those variables is necessary to make the initial comparison interpretable. If transit-through-sprites itself feels unacceptable, record it and stop; do not silently add swept projectile collision to one arm.

## 11. What the comparison can establish

F0→N0: effect of control mapping, correction, acquisition/leading, release semantics and minimum aim feedback **as a bundle**, with the same encounter and ordinary rendering/collision family. Coordinate-derived N `pEnd` can differ from F selected-target depth: T, sprite scale and shadow metadata need not match even for a similar intended actor. Preserve/disclose this confound, never alter F to equalize it. N neutral sling tilt also differs from F gesture tilt. Automatic leading, hover commitment, F guide, N reticle, N legal area and miss continuation are known differences, not equalized-away details.

N0→N1: added support/cart opportunity with input held fixed. Support outline, one-time support instruction, brake cue, causal animation, altered number/timing of send-homes and strategic opportunity necessarily differ. This is not a pure mass/physics-engine experiment. Repetition, an unprompted timing-transfer attempt, local intent explanations and preference after repetition reduce the novelty explanation; they cannot eliminate it or prove retention. If only N1 is liked but N0 fails control gates, input improvement has not been shown.

Advance only under the preregistered gates in [validation](PROTOTYPE-AND-VALIDATION-PLAN.md). Unknowns requiring actual testing: point-plane comprehension, finger occlusion, moving-target leading, N aim-area adequacy, direct-shot versus setup timing, appeal after repetition, access without holding, and actual modest-device stability. These are uncertainties to measure, not permission to leave different implementations unspecified.
