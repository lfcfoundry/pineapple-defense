# Levels and progression — R2 scope and preservation plan

2026-11-17 · Documentation only. The [independent review](INDEPENDENT-DESIGN-REVIEW-2026-11-17.md) rejected treating a complete physics campaign as justified. R1's 25 new wave briefs, Champion rigs, new economy and material curriculum are withdrawn as an implementation plan. They are not needed to test the core hypothesis. [Minimum specification](MINIMUM-EXPERIMENT-SPEC.md) defines the only initial encounter.

## One encounter, one timing-transfer preset

A stand faces four basic Rockhoppers. Three form the rear group near the cart's rail; one approaches on the opposite side nearer the stand. The player may rescue directly, clear the group with individual shots, or time one support release. The cart and support are visibly parked in F0/N0 and available in N1. There is no sequence of environments, escalating projectile roster, reward shop or campaign unlock in the experiment.

Preset A supplies practice and early learning; preset B changes rear/urgent arrival timing in the same layout for transfer. All geometry, rules, types and rewards remain constant. Exact positions, speeds and termination rules are in the specification. Their intended tactical reversal is a qualification hypothesis, not an achieved encounter result. A configuration where direct fire always dominates or the support always wins fails; adding more objects is not its remedy.

A defensive completion means no walking actors remain by the source-quantized Outcome boundary (§9); scoring ends there without waiting for reactions. Four actual send-homes with zero boops is a clean defense; mistakes are recorded without losing currency or ownership. Retry resets the full input/timing/fixture manifest in spec §9. Recovery requires clean defense after its two fixed early misses (§7); four passive boops cannot pass. Use separated R3 rear coordinates and unchanged source radii. Meaningful improvement is anticipating when the cart meets the group and reprioritizing when the urgent threat arrives sooner, not unlocking higher damage or memorizing a support highlight.

## Existing game systems: preserve while researching

The application is unchanged. The table separates what the current game retains from what the isolated fixture includes; exclusion from a research fixture does not remove an existing feature.

| Five waves per stage | Preserve | One encounter; no wave generator or campaign advancement |

## Future progression is conditional, not specified by this experiment

If N0 wins without N1, improve authored defense before assuming physics is needed. If N1 adds a reliable, repeated decision, evaluate one further variation with the same vocabulary before a campaign rewrite. Broad projectile roles, different materials, moving gates, hinge chains and new Champion structures need separate evidence and design approval. Do not turn all Champions into the same support puzzle to reuse a prototype.

No price or comfort unlock is proposed. Any later economy redesign must preserve earned ownership, define refunds/equivalent utility, separate legacy best scores and map every save field. `PD1` text compatibility alone is not a migration. Checkpoint restart semantics must explicitly cover already-earned rewards, consumed specials, encounter version/seed and durable recovery before any new campaign writes progress. These decisions can safely wait because this experiment has no progression writes.

## Open content questions

Only testing can show whether one cart creates enough choice, whether the preset reversal is legible and recoverable, and whether repeated direct shots remain a viable but meaningfully different route. Whether that vocabulary can sustain five stages is a later research question. No claim about campaign length, retention or replay value follows from one successful encounter.
