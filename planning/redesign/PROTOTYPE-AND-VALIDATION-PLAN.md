# Prototype and validation plan — reconciled R3

2026-11-17 · **Plan only. No prototype, player test, QA suite, build or benchmark has run.** All numbers are proposed acceptance gates. The [minimum experiment specification](MINIMUM-EXPERIMENT-SPEC.md) is the sole implementation contract; this file owns study procedure and go/no-go interpretation. The [independent review](INDEPENDENT-DESIGN-REVIEW-2026-11-17.md) required a smaller, falsifiable comparison.

## Matched control conditions

1. Does the new point-control contract improve intent reliability, understanding and perceived control over the original flick?
2. With that input unchanged, does one support/cart opportunity add a repeated, transferable defensive decision?

Use one encounter and three conditions, exactly as specified:

| Comparison | Held consistent | Intervention / admitted confound |
|---|---|---|
| F0 original flick → N0 point aim | Same four actors, timing preset, scene, braked props, Classic rendering/collision family and reload, comfort settings | Mapping, correction, release, automatic leading, N legal area, miss continuation and minimum aim feedback, neutral N sling tilt and point-derived flight-time/scale/shadow metadata differ; this measures the control bundle, not finger movement alone |
| N0 → N1 interactive cart | Exactly the same new input, ordinary shot behavior, actors, geometry, timing and absence of rewards | Normal support outline, one-time support instruction, brake/available cue, support release, cart motion and defensive opportunity necessarily differ. This measures the useful causal toy, not a pure physical-force effect |

Do not use F0 versus N1 alone to claim input improvement. If N0 loses but N1 excites, improved input is unproven. The original candidate corridor, scoring, launch-time leading, target-dependent range branch and original guide **stay intact in F0**. No purportedly fair removal of snap, no shared velocity-only adapter and no replacement first-contact predictor for F.

No pullback, alternate projectile, general physics solver, separate environment, Champion or campaign implementation is needed. A non-hold/keyboard N adaptation supports access and is identified separately; F's lack of keyboard aiming is a baseline capability gap, not a reason to force timed arrow gestures.

## Before participant testing: qualification gates

After separate future implementation approval, qualify the isolated artifact before collecting human preference:

- Pin and record source SHA, artifact identity, browser/version, viewport and condition settings. Prove that raw traces through F0 produce the same eligibility, recent segment, speed, selected target, endpoint, time, arc, soft flag, extension, cooldown, pouch origin and tilt as the pinned source under identical state/dt inputs. Include two candidates on a ray, a stronger/slower flick selecting the same actor, a no-candidate shot, a vanished target, early release and desktop hover. Any silent replacement of original selection invalidates the comparison.
- Verify that N stationary release and correction back to the starting point are valid; interruption/cancel cannot launch. Check source-based F defects without correcting the control arm during the study.
- Verify N ring and landing endpoint agreement independently of shared code. Confirm that neither arm resolves airborne sprite crossings and that the caption makes the landing convention explicit.
- Qualify preset A/B geometry: both controls can complete directly with a useful deadline margin, the two fixed injected misses in spec §7 allow clean A defense under each control, and support timing yields genuinely different useful priorities in B. A zero-margin theoretical solution is insufficient. If these fail, make one documented tuning pass and freeze the revised spec before human comparisons; do not add content.
- Statically project the R3 coordinates at all four spec §7 canvas sizes, both presets: disjoint initial sprite rectangles/actor disks, exposed support disk and center in N area. Keep source radii. Verify §2 dependency exclusions leave no interactive trophy/pickup/scene state; no demo/bot shortcut. Verify §9 reset and pre-GO fresh-input gating. Check cart release/stop/boop ties against §8.
- Confirm exactly one support/cart, no reward differential or forced watch period, legal extra shots during cart travel, no real-save reads/writes, and offline operation. Test lower-detail/Calm without changing results.

No gate above has been executed. The qualification list is future work, not a test suite created in this documentation task.

## Participants and sequence

Use engineering shakedown participants first, never counted toward gates. Then recruit exactly 12 novice primary touch participants: 6 children aged 7–10 and 6 adults aged 18+, none previously exposed to this fixture or playing Pineapple Defense more than one hour. Guardian consent/child assent; anonymous local notes/consented exports only. All twelve use direct press/release and the same phone/input settings per session. Keyboard/non-hold and mouse/touchpad sessions form separate descriptive cohorts; recruit at least one tester per supported adaptation/device path, never pool their error rates or preference into the 12. Record handedness/comfort and explain if this narrow sample limits generality.

Split into two short sessions or offer breaks between blocks; 15–20 minutes is a target, never permission to omit unfavorable scored trials. All blocks use the same encounter; no fourth condition is added. Neutral labels X/Y are assigned per pair, not “old/new/better.”

| Primary IDs (enrollment order) | Stage 1, before any N1 exposure | Stage 2, after primary judgment locked |
|---|---|---|
| 1,5,9 | F0 then N0 | N0 then N1 |
| 2,6,10 | N0 then F0 | N1 then N0 |
| 3,7,11 | F0 then N0 | N1 then N0 |
| 4,8,12 | N0 then F0 | N0 then N1 |

Assign child IDs 1–6 and adult IDs 7–12 (three per order within each stage per age group). Stage 1 and 2 each have two blocks. Every block: fixed instruction ≤20 s, then at most 60 active seconds A practice (finish current attempt only if within cap), then exactly three scored A attempts. Reset thr=0 **at block start**, retaining it through practice/retries. Repeat N0 as a fresh block in stage 2; this is the same condition, not a new arm. Stage-1 ratings/preference, first three understanding items and intended-point probes are recorded before any N1 presentation, then the primary input judgment is locked; later data cannot rescue N0. Re-practice/prior N exposure and unequal total exposure are reported, not concealed by counterbalancing.

Fixed scripts (read once, no extra performance coaching):

- Common: “Send the penguins home. Keep the stand cheerful. You can retry.”
- F0: “Flick up toward a penguin. A harder flick throws farther. Cart parked.” Retain source guide and authentic targeting, even where that simple source instruction omits prediction details.
- N0: “Press where it should land, move to correct if needed, lift to throw. Penguins keep moving. Cart parked.”
- N1: same N sentence, then “Hit the support to release the cart.”
- Access: replace lift sentence with “Choose the landing point, then use Fire”; demonstrate labeled controls once, no solution coaching.

After stage-2 blocks and their ratings/preference, run one unprompted B in N1, then show only its actual outcome (boops/send-homes), ask “What would you change?”, and run B again. No suggested target/timing. Both B trials retain normal conditional support outline; omit teaching text/arrows/emphasis only. Ask checklist items 4–5 before first B without teaching the answer; items 1–3 were already recorded at the end of stage 1. Final optional A round: choose N0, N1 or neither, no reward. Then sample the recovery probe for IDs 1,3,5,7,9,11 in both F0/N0, order matching their stage 1; identical injected misses and no route coaching. Do not add sampled recovery into primary error/preference estimates. Record fatigue/refusal; split session instead of substituting passive completion.

Access cohorts: up to 60 active seconds practice per declared N input adaptation, then three scored A attempts in N0. Mandatory task is A clean defense (four actual send-homes, zero boops), at least once in three; B is not an access requirement. State support/needs individually. Inability to learn is an outcome, never an exclusion. Explicit interruptions follow spec §9: keep record and one allowed replacement, otherwise missing. Withdrawals/missing slots cannot create a pass: retain the original 12-person denominator for count gates; label incomplete paired data inconclusive, not favorable. No participant is replaced for poor performance.

## Fixed instruments and coding (E4)

Event log: condition/block/preset/attempt IDs, starting thr, viewport/settings, input receipt times, pointer ownership/coordinates, intended action where known, acceptance/rejection reason, selected F target, actual endpoint/T/arc/soft, launch/contact/boop/cart-release/stop times, contact IDs, cancellations, interruptions and Outcome snapshot. No live tracking outside the isolated local study.

- Code each human launch intention as accepted launch, early-cooldown rejection, invalid gesture/acquisition, wrong endpoint/actor, or accidental launch; allow diagnostic subcodes and multiple causes, but count at most one failed intention in comparative rates. Intentional cancellation is separate; unknown intent remains unknown. Confirm intention in immediate post-attempt event review without suggesting an answer; never infer intention from success. An attempt at firing that never launches is still an intention. An accepted miss on a knowingly chosen empty point is not parser failure. Accidental launches are extra commits denied by the participant, not “all misses.”
- Human launch reliability = accepted deliberate launches / known intended launches, ≥90% for N, with cancellations excluded from numerator/denominator. Report per participant and pooled; report early rejects and aiming errors separately (acceptance alone is not hitting intent). Safety: zero unintended launch in scripted owner-cancel, capture loss, window/composite blur, hidden tab, resize, modal, pause and pre-GO cases; internal Tab must preserve keyboard point. Scripted probes are engineering qualification, not human hit rates.
- Comparative failure rate per participant = `(failed known launch intentions + accidental launches) / (known launch intentions + accidental launches)` in the three stage-1 scored A slots. Failed known launch means wrong intended actor/known endpoint or rejected intended launch; accidental commits are the separate added term and never counted twice. Wrong endpoint is classifiable only when intent was independently recorded, not inferred from the reticle; unknown point intent stays unknown. Deduplicate event errors. Mean paired rates give F and N estimates; reduction `(meanF−meanN)/meanF`. Require ≥30% when F has ≥10 failures across ≥4 people. Unknown-intent share and missing slots are reported per arm; >10% unknown events in either arm or any missing paired participant makes this gate inconclusive. Noncompleters' valid attempts stay in counts. Zero-intention/no-play is not zero error: mark rate undefined and gate inconclusive, retaining defensive failures. For sparse F errors require N no worse paired mean plus comprehension/control gates, with no repeated new N failure category in ≥2 people. Child/input subgroup regression is an escalation flag requiring review, not an invented statistical pass: report paired child rates and all descriptive access cohorts.
- Human intended-point precision is a diagnostic probe after stage-1 scored preference/understanding and before N1 exposure, not reticle-to-endpoint tautology. Give one such GO-snapshot probe per participant per F0/N0 in their stage-1 order, naming ID2 as the intended actor. Freeze actor state only for independent intention marking; restart at the identical GO fixture, live after countdown for actual action, preserving that block’s final thr. Exclude these probes from learning, defense and comparative failure counts. Ask participant to mark intended landing point on an unannotated paused snapshot before executing its action; store coordinates/timestamp and intended actor ID independently of the hidden actual reticle. Then restore identical state and let them act; mark this a cued probe, outside live-defense rates. Normalize endpoint error by `2*0.55*psz(p)` of declared actor at intention timestamp. Exclude overlapping actors from precision only. Unknown actor/point is missing, reported. Engineering stationary ring→endpoint error ≤4 CSS px is a separate check. Cued probes cannot prove natural live leading; live actor hit/error rates supply that evidence.
- Fixed checklist questions (items 1–3 after stage-1 preference; 4–5 before first B): “Does the landing point move if a penguin walks away?”, “Can a penguin move before it lands?”, “Does crossing a penguin in the air always count?”, “What if a penguin is on the support point when it lands?”, “What makes the cart move and what happens when it meets a walking penguin?” Score one binary item each: fixed coordinate, live movement, transit noncollision, penguin-before-support priority, support→cart→send-home. Record first unprompted answer then any prompted answer separately; only unprompted scores pass.
- After equal three scored A exposures in each block ask “How much could you make shots do what you intended?” (1 never, 2 rarely, 3 sometimes, 4 usually, 5 always) and “How enjoyable was defending this round?” (1 not at all, 2 slightly, 3 somewhat, 4 very, 5 extremely). Record stage-specific ratings. After each pair ask “Which control/round would you choose again: X, Y, or no preference?” Reasons are recorded verbatim as qualitative explanations, never a retrospective veto on numerical gates. Replay choice agreement means ≥8/12 choose N1, including ≥4/6 children; if preference passes but replay does not, classify cart appeal mixed/inconclusive, not a pass.
- Freeze tactics before participants: A setup-first = first accepted launch at support, B urgent-first = first accepted launch resolving on ID4 before its boop. Useful cart timing = support release produces ≥2 cart send-homes with zero boops overall; contact-time windows must be calculated/recorded during future qualification against the frozen dt schedules. Reversal qualification requires clean setup-first A and clean urgent-first B routes, with ≥0.15 s latest-safe-first-action margin in both controls for direct routes and in N1 for chosen setup routes. No claim that the original layout already passes. Individual transfer success = useful A setup during scored N1 followed by urgent-first B and a useful delayed release on first B. Require ≥8/12; second B improvement is reported separately, never substituted for first-B transfer. If A cannot demonstrate setup use, that participant fails the transfer gate; do not assume moving actors prove lead mastery.


## Measures and proposed acceptance gates

Use these gates from the review with explicit domain boundaries. Counts such as 10/12 and 8/12 are formative decision rules, not statistical significance or achieved results. Lock the study revision and analysis definitions before testing.

| Domain | Proposed go criterion | Reject / diagnose |
|---|---|---|
| Input learning | ≥10/12 including ≥5/6 children achieve three intentional eligible target landings within 60 s of practice after ≤20 s instruction, using repeated A if needed | Repeated coaching or a move-away activation trick is necessary; report F/N separately |
| Input reliability | ≥90% of known deliberate N launch intentions accepted (cancellations separate), using the coding above; 0 unintended N launches on scripted cancel/blur/capture-loss/resize/modal cases | Separate acquisition, activation, parser, cooldown rejection, aiming error and collision timing. Script reliability is not human hit rate. |
| Improvement over actual F | N0 reduces paired intent-related failure rates by ≥30% when F0 has at least 10 such errors across at least 4 participants; report subgroup escalation under the fixed coding rule | If F0 errors are sparse, use the explicit sparse-baseline rule above; do not manufacture a percentage from 1–2 errors |
| Point predictability (primary input) | ≥10/12 including ≥5/6 children pass first three unprompted checklist items before N1 | A target-following expectation persists or players interpret transit overlap as guaranteed contact |
| Landing precision | On declared intended-point shots, median endpoint placement error ≤0.35 intended actor contact diameter at intention timestamp; stationary ring→endpoint discrepancy ≤4 CSS px | Distinguish human placement from engineering endpoint error; do not compare world units to screen px at different apparent depths |
| Causal understanding | ≥10/12 including ≥5/6 children pass fourth and fifth unprompted checklist items before first B; normal contact outline remains | Ring reading alone or “it just happens” is not understanding; simplify before adding physics |
| Satisfaction / perceived control | After repetition, median N0 perceived-control rating ≥4/5 and no lower than F0; ≥8/12 including ≥4/6 children prefer N0's control; reasons are explanatory only | Higher hit rate due to a changed collider or more spectacle cannot pass; confusing minority outcomes remain reported |
| Cart appeal beyond novelty | After ≥3 A repetitions, ≥8/12 including ≥4/6 children prefer N1 over N0 and optional extra-round choice meets the same count threshold | First-show excitement alone, bigger reward, or a forced watch period does not pass; mixed evidence warrants another narrow study |
| Skill and meaningful choice | Direct and setup routes are both viable; compare boops first, then launches for equally safe completions. ≥8/12 meet first-B transfer definition above; second-B improvement separately reported | Same first action regardless of threat, cart always wins, or spam always wins. Faster execution alone is not skill transfer. |
| Recoverability and fairness | Clean A defense remains possible after the fixed two injected misses; ≥80% of sampled learners in each control achieve clean recovery; report failed intended actors and observed boop timing (qualitative fairness account) | Fixed odd-ID sample; learners are those meeting three intentional hits within practice cap in that control. Report numerator/denominator; zero eligible learners is inconclusive. Clean defense required, never passive completion |
| Access | Every recruited N keyboard/non-hold tester achieves clean preset A in at least one of three attempts after the declared practice without a speed gesture/timed chord; no essential comfort unlock | Report capability and needs individually; pooled adult mouse success cannot mask exclusion |
| Performance floor | Predeclared modest phone: p95 frame interval ≤33.3 ms and event-receipt→drawn N point response ≤33.3 ms over three short attempts; no recurring automatic pauses, no unbounded list growth | 60 FPS is a stretch goal, 30 FPS the proposed floor. Label actual browser/device/settings; rAF timing is not touch-to-photon. |
| Simulation stability | Same dt/input stream gives same contact IDs/order and outcomes; stationary endpoint error ≤4 CSS px; cart sweep misses no crossing at 30/60/120 render schedules outside declared edge ties | Report inherited frame-quantized endpoint differences separately. New cart misses/hitches cannot be excused as baseline behavior. Analytic cart position/sweep tolerance ≤0.5 CSS px is an independent geometry target. |
| Isolation | Zero runtime requests, no new runtime dependency, zero reads/writes to real progress; no changes in outcome from DPR/Calm | Reject artifact before participants if isolation fails. These are future checks, not current measurements. |

Before shakedown, record the actual available modest-phone make/model, OS, approved test browser/version and all four canvas qualification sizes; nominate 360×640 for the primary portrait session. No named device is presumed available or measured. An unfilled device manifest blocks performance qualification/player study, not implementation. Use project-approved browsers only, rechecking its required browser instructions before any future run. Frame p95 uses all foreground Active frame intervals in three consecutive A attempts per arm (nearest-rank percentile); exclude Preparing/Countdown/Outcome and interrupted attempts, report exclusions/hitches separately. N response latency pairs each latest received point with the first rendered frame carrying it; superseded points are explicitly marked/coalesced rather than treated as displayed. Report coalesced-event count/fraction and maximum unpainted interval alongside p95; a dropped point cannot create a fictional fast response. Record rAF/draw completion as software proxies, not observed photons. Check list counts return to reset baseline after each attempt; any retained-per-attempt growth fails. Independent analytic/dense-reference circle-contact calculations must check release/stop/boop ties, IDs/order and crossings at 30/60/120 schedules, without calling production sweep; ≤0.5 px geometry acceptance, ≤1e−7 s tie band as spec §8. Exact same stream requires same result; cross-refresh source endpoint quantization remains separately reported.

Physical touch-to-photon latency can be measured externally in a later targeted check; do not label event-receipt timing as display latency. Retain the review's possible p95 ≤80 ms physical-response goal only when the measurement setup is available. Do not convert a missing high-speed recording into a fabricated pass.

## Distinguishing input, understanding, learning and novelty

F0/N0 provides no interactive cart difference, so new-physics excitement cannot explain an input-bundle preference there. Record whether N's lack of automatic leading raises moving-target errors even if stationary point placement improves. A readable point that people cannot use in defense is not a control win.

N0/N1 holds N input fixed. It necessarily changes physical affordance, cart animation and potential clear efficiency, so it cannot establish that a numerical physics solver itself causes fun. Repeated play, transfer, stated intent and the optional extra round test whether the effect survives discovery. No short study proves long-term retention. A constrained authored rail is acceptable if it supplies readable agency; this study does not justify general rigid bodies.

Plot each participant's errors/choices by repetition and timing preset. Include counts of rejected early releases, accidental launches, direct shots, support attempts, cart send-homes, boops, completion times, corrections, and pauses. Count an intentional cancellation separately from a rejected intended launch. Report negative transfer: pointing at current moving bodies rather than leading may be a real N weakness.

## Decisions after evidence

- **N0 fails:** stop or revise the diagnosed control problem once, then repeat a targeted comparison. Do not rescue the recommendation with N1 preference.
- **N0 passes; N1 fails:** favor dependable authored defense without interactive props. The full playground has not earned development.
- **Both pass:** authorize only a separately reviewed next question; do not infer a validated campaign, second projectile or general physics engine.
- **Technical/access hard gate fails:** fix or simplify before further players/content, even if pooled preference passes.

Record source/artifact revision, instruction/order, participant session codes, actual browser/version/viewport/device, comfort/input mode, preset, all attempts including failures, exclusions with reasons and confounds. Never claim “the gesture is better” when evidence supports only a control-feedback bundle. Leave disagreement visible and record empirical outcomes in the decision log only after real evidence exists.
