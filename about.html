What it is and how it works

A silly, glossy toy-box game for kids of all ages. Penguins march toward your fruit stand and you flick a pineapple from the bottom of the screen: the direction picks the target area, the speed sets the power. It is one self-contained HTML file with no network calls, and it works on phones, tablets and computers.

Five stages, five Champions

Each Champion fights differently, and a hint tells you how to win.

Pineapple Roll

A carnival bonus game after stage 4, with holes that score more the harder they are.

21 trophies

Trophies pay Golden Pineapples to spend in the Shop.

Comfortable settings

Left-handed layout, calm mode, larger text, high contrast and a battery saver.

2.4 MB

The whole game is one HTML file: all art, sounds and code, no network calls (2,533,166 bytes after the last picture re-pack).

113 ms

From page load to a finished start-up in Chromium (median of 9 runs; it was 140 ms before the last trim).

6.1 ms

Page code time to start the next stage, from 10.0 ms when the next background is drawn ahead of time (stages 2 to 5, Chromium).

3 browsers

Every release passes the full check in Chromium, Firefox and WebKitGTK, in about 145 seconds.

Measured on the project's test machines at normal speed and logged in the speed history. Phones vary, and the FPS counter in Settings tells you in plain words how yours is doing.

Stages and Champions

Five stages, five waves each, then a Champion who fights their own way. A hint tells you how to win. After stage 4 you can play Pineapple Roll, a carnival bonus game. Each full run makes penguins and Champions 10% faster, up to double speed, with fresh challenges and more trophies. There are 21 trophies in all, and a few things in the scenery are worth poking at.

Upgrades to spend your pineapples on

Auto Slingshot

A helper slingshot on your table that tosses pineapples for you and sometimes misses.

Faster Fling

Three levels: your helper tosses every 5.2 seconds, then 4.2, 3.4 and 2.8.

Better Pineapples

Unlocks the splashy Lobber, then the super-splashy Mega pineapple.

Lime Launcher

A small green helper for Champion fights that pops shields and snowballs. More Limes and Lots of Limes raise it to 12 and then 20 limes a fight.

Gull Pals

Three levels: a gull snatches falling fish, so fewer penguins level up.

Paper Umbrellas

Three levels: umbrellas on your table snap open and fling away a penguin about to bump you.

Pineapple Rain

Two levels: press Rain and a row of pineapples falls across the lanes.

Beyond the basics

Combos

Every hit within a second and a half chains, whether it comes from your sling, a helper or one big splash.

Smart penguins

A penguin that eats a parachuting fish levels up and gets faster and tougher. A Gentoo that eats another becomes a mecha penguin with armor that takes 3 hits.

Power-ups

Zippy for super fast throws, Mega so every throw splashes, Toasty for sleepy penguins, Cheer up to refill the meter.

Made for everyone

Left-handed layout, calm mode, larger text and buttons, high contrast, a battery saver, tap-or-hold throwing for very young players, and a one-tap Little hands setup.

Safe by design

No ads, purchases, accounts, streaks, timers or leaderboards. Progress stays in your browser, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

Why it exists

LFC Foundry is a home for free, open source games and apps that each live in a single file. Pineapple Defense is the flicking game in that set, made for kids first: no ads, purchases, accounts, streaks, timers or leaderboards, and losing is gentle.
